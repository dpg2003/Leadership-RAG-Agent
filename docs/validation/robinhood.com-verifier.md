## Pipeline bugs suspected (patterns, not one-offs)
1. **is_current=false inferred from absence in the proxy.** The 2026-04-22 proxy lists only named executive officers, so absence is not evidence of departure. 11 roles use this rule (Chennapragada, Bhatt x3, Howard COO, Southwick, Smedley, Brown, Modica, Ashkenas, Zigaitis, Kerbrat CTO). The outcome is probably true for most but unsupported by any text. Only Warnick, Pinner and Hoge have an explicit departure statement. Use "unknown"/as_of rather than false, or require explicit evidence.
2. **Stale single-source roles left is_current=true** (Wolff 2020, Seigle 2021, Brown VP 2021). The pipeline notes the staleness in `note` but still outputs true, and these count toward people_current=25. Three of 25 "current" people are not supported as current (12% error in current headcount).
3. **Rejected evidence silently corrupts as_of/currency.** Bhatt's 2024-03-21 release lists him as CCO and Co-Founder but the claim was rejected ("evidence does not state an affiliation with Robinhood"), so the newest as_of is 2022 and he looks departed. The affiliation check is too strict for press-release boilerplate where the company is implied by the page.
4. **Title selection prefers older/higher-priority source over the newest title.** Gallagher: stored "Chief Legal Officer" despite the 2026 proxy and investors page giving the longer current title (company_site priority beats sec_filing regardless of date). Recency should outweigh source priority for titles.
5. **Undated S-1/A (2021) treated as current.** Its title facts (Tenev, Bhatt, Smedley, etc.) have published_at=null and is_current=true even though the content is 2021 (ages 34/36/44...). Search-source documents need a date or an is_current=null default.
6. **Fact-level is_current stays true on superseded facts** (e.g. Pinner 2024 release/proxy facts true while the 8-K says separated). Role-level resolution is correct but the fact rows are misleading; review block "resolved: false" for Pinner, Warnick and Hoge is out of date.
7. **Bio and location extraction quality.** Bio picks anecdotes or prior-employer sentences (Tenev "born in Bulgaria", Bazzano's Gap years); location "San Francisco" from an interviewer's line.
8. **Blocked host dependence.** 12 of the 25 current people have investors.robinhood.com (403) as their only live-verifiable source; cached text supports them, but they need a browser re-check before release.
